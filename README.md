# Themia

Widgets for the Windows desktop: clock, weather, calendar, mail, tasks, system meters and more,
drawn on your wallpaper behind your windows.

![Themia widgets on a Windows 11 desktop, above the taskbar](docs/readme/desktop.png)

## Get it

From the Microsoft Store, for Windows 11 ([themia.app](https://themia.app) has the link). Free for up
to 20 widgets and 2 layouts; Themia Pro, a one-time purchase, removes both limits.

Every contribution gets you a free Themia Pro licence: a bug report, an idea, a translation fix,
testing a fix, helping someone in the issues, or code. Mention it in your issue or pull request, or
email [support@themia.app](mailto:support@themia.app) with a link.
